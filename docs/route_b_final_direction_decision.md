# Route B passive-theory direction decision

Date: 2026-10-20  
Literature verdict: **INCONCLUSIVE-BLOCKED**  
Implementation decision: **NO-GO**

The current syndrome and joint-window results are mathematically useful as
boundary analysis, but they do not yet establish an independent contribution
over classical fault isolation and secure estimation. The last permitted
passive-route activity is the frozen full-text audit in
`route_b_final_fulltext_audit_protocol.md`.

That audit cannot currently be completed because the required primary full
texts are neither present locally nor reachable through the managed cloud
proxy. This is not a finding that Route B is novel, and it is not a finding that
the literature has already closed the problem.

Until the evidence matrix is complete:

- do not implement D-MIR;
- do not add further syndrome, code-distance or event-set theorem variants;
- do not describe the current theorem package as a paper contribution;
- keep RCL and all negative results frozen;
- do not begin a simulator or tune a passive attribution method.

If the completed audit returns NO-GO, archive the passive fault-isolation-style
route. The proposed pivot is then only a new scoping question: whether active
evidence verification under unknown attack strategy, verification cost and
identifiability constraints contains a defensible gap beyond controlled
sensing, active hypothesis testing, information-gain planning and POMDPs. It
must pass its own prior-art gate before any algorithm is developed.
