# True/induced-region intent-coupled RCL development

Primary matched controls: standard hypothesis-independent RCL and a latent-surface mixture with the same components but H-independent surface weights. All share reliable emissions, marginal cell contamination rates, primary temperature, fixed commit step and sensing costs. F is an induced region plus a concealment fallback state, not a complete fake operational hypothesis. Surface components use all observed reports during presentation-event cycles; this fitting change is shared by the unconditional control. The conditional relation can encode red-script regularities. Reversed decoy policies are separate stress tests, never included in primary overall figures.
